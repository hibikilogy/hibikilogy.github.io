<div style="text-align: center;"><strong>This Band Is Your Band</strong></div>

<div style="text-align: right;">For Kitauji High school Concert Band<br>（献给北宇治高中吹奏部）</div>

&emsp;&emsp;From the Flute and Piccolo to the Drum and Marimba
